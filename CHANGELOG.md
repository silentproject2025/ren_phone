## Unreleased
- Improved: smooth transitions between the screen and the Always-On Display, for both the lock screen and Home (AOD_FX_V1). Going to AOD, the last frame now fades to black over 0.9 s while the backlight steps down (stepped in perceived brightness, so it feels even), then the AOD clock fades in. Waking by touch renders the live screen once and fades it in over 0.3 s while the backlight comes up and the AOD clock fades out; waking in the middle of the fade starts from the current brightness instead of jumping. The AOD face is new and the same on lock screen and Home: clock, date, notification count and Wi-Fi IP, all in dim grey. The fade keeps one screenshot (about 300 KB) in PSRAM only while it runs; if PSRAM is too low it falls back to the old instant switch.
